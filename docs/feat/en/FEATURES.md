# Commercial Features & Capabilities Overview

## Executive Summary

**Daely Report Studio** is an all-in-one, commercial-grade student evaluation and progress reporting platform engineered specifically for educational institutions, K-12 learning centers, STEM & coding academies, language institutes, and private tutoring organizations.

The platform bridges the communication gap between educators and parents by transforming raw, informal teaching notes into polished, articulate, and brand-consistent progress reports in seconds. Equipped with an interactive web portal for families and centralized branch administration tools, the platform elevates an institution's service quality, drives parent satisfaction, and saves instructors hundreds of hours of administrative overhead every month.

---

## Key Business Value & ROI

- **Up to 90% Reduction in Administrative Time:** Instructors can generate complete, multi-paragraph evaluation reports in under one minute per student—down from 15–20 minutes of manual drafting.
- **Consistent Pedagogical Quality Across All Staff:** Guarantees that every report sent to parents meets the institution's professional standards and vocabulary, regardless of the individual teacher’s writing skill or tenure.
- **Stronger Parent Engagement & Retention:** Delights parents with transparent, engaging digital reports and visual learning evidence, significantly increasing student re-enrollment rates and word-of-mouth referrals.
- **Zero-Friction Access for Families:** Parents access their child's records through secure, interactive web links without needing to install mobile apps, remember usernames, or navigate complex sign-in forms.
- **Turnkey White-Label Scalability:** Fully adaptable to any school or tutoring franchise brand identity, complete with multi-branch management and staff governance.

---

## Core Capabilities & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Intelligent AI-Powered Report Generation
- **Automated Narrative Synthesis:** Instantly converts brief lesson bullet points, topics covered, and behavioral observations into comprehensive, grammatically flawless student evaluations.
- **Pedagogically Structured Reports:** Every generated report follows a standardized, four-pillar framework designed to give parents clarity:
  1. *Lesson Overview & Milestones:* What concept or project was tackled during the session.
  2. *Teacher’s Observational Notes:* How the student engaged, focused, overcame challenges, and participated.
  3. *Progressive Practice & Training Recommendations:* Tailored suggestions for homework, exercises, or developmental games to practice before the next session.
  4. *Parent Guidance Note:* Practical advice for parents on how to support and encourage their child at home.
- **Hands-Free Voice Dictation (Speech-to-Text):** Instructors on the go can dictate notes directly using their microphone right after class ends, eliminating the need to type out lengthy paragraphs on small screens.
- **Flexible Reporting Modes:** 
  - *Full Comprehensive Report:* In-depth evaluation with complete observations, practice recommendations, and parent tips.
  - *Summary Only:* High-level snapshot ideal for rapid check-ins or quick weekly updates.
  - *Manual Composition:* Full freedom for educators who prefer to type or fine-tune reports manually.
- **Automated Lesson Counting:** Tracks meeting numbers and chronological lesson continuity automatically, preventing duplicate or skipped meeting records.
- **Image Proof & Learning Artifact Attachments:** Upload and attach photos of the student's project, workbook, screen, or whiteboard to provide visual evidence of achievements.

---

### 2. Brand & Writing Style Customization (Style Datasets)
- **Custom Institutional Tone Training:** Train the AI assistant to mirror your organization's unique pedagogical style, terminology, and brand voice (e.g., encouraging, rigorous, gamified, or academic).
- **Curated Practice Recommendation Catalog:** Create and organize a repository of approved practice ideas and home exercises organized by skill category:
  - *Creative Exploration & Projects*
  - *Structured Logic & Problem Solving*
  - *Experimental & Discovery-Based Tasks*
  - *Foundational Skills & Core Concepts*
- **One-Click Starter Kits:** Includes pre-built, professional templates and recommendation presets, enabling new branches or instructors to start generating quality reports immediately on day one.

---

### 3. Student Roster & Schedule Management
- **Centralized Student Directory:** Maintain complete student profiles including enrolled subjects, grade levels, active instructors, and cumulative meeting tallies.
- **Flexible Class Scheduling:** Group students into session schedules with specific lesson days, start and end times, and campus/classroom labels.
- **Individual Student Language Preferences:** Configure preferred reporting languages (English or Indonesian) on a per-student basis so international families receive reports in their language of choice.
- **Seamless Student Transfer & Handover:** Reassign students and their complete historical report portfolios between instructors with zero lost records—ideal for teacher transitions, leave coverage, or class reallocations.

---

### 4. Interactive Parent Web Portal
- **Dedicated Student Web Link:** Every enrolled student receives a unique, branded web address that parents can bookmark and revisit at any time.
- **Modern, Consumer-Grade Interface:** Clean, welcoming, and mobile-friendly design that showcases the school's commitment to technology and transparency.
- **Visual Learning Gallery:** Highlights session photos and milestone achievements in high-definition with full-screen preview capabilities.
- **Complete Chronological Learning Archive:** Families can browse through all past meetings to witness long-term learning curves, skill development, and instructor observations over months or years.
- **One-Click Sharing:** Enables parents and educators to quickly copy the formatted report text or share links directly through WhatsApp and messaging channels.
- **Direct Two-Way Parent Feedback:** An integrated feedback channel built right into the portal, allowing parents to send appreciation, questions, or updates directly to the teaching staff.

---

### 5. Parent Feedback Inbox & Retention Hub
- **Unified Feedback Inbox:** A dedicated dashboard for instructors and administrators to read and manage incoming feedback from parents.
- **Unread Status Tracking:** Automatically highlights newly submitted feedback to ensure no parent inquiry goes unnoticed.
- **Customer Satisfaction Loop:** Allows branch managers to monitor parent sentiment, address concerns proactively, and acknowledge positive feedback.

---

### 6. Historical Report Archives & Intelligence
- **Global Search & Filter Engine:** Instantly search across the entire institutional archive by student name, subject, lesson topic, or observation keyword.
- **Dynamic Time-Period Filtering:** Review reports by specific timeframes:
  - *This Week* (ideal for weekly progress audits)
  - *This Month*
  - *Last Month*
  - *All Time*
- **Post-Publication Editing:** Instructors can easily revise historical reports, update meeting dates, or replace learning photos if corrections are needed.
- **Batch Sharing & WhatsApp Formatter:** Pre-formats multiple reports or portal links into clean, emoji-accented message templates ready to be sent to parent groups.

---

### 7. Multi-Instructor & Enterprise Administration
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
  - *Branch Administrator:* Complete oversight over all instructors, institutional settings, student rosters, audit logs, and feedback.
  - *Instructor / Teacher:* Focused view restricted to their assigned students, schedules, reports, and personal profile.
- **Controlled Staff Onboarding:** Invite instructors securely via email invitations or one-time activation links, ensuring only authorized personnel access institutional records.
- **Safe Staff Offboarding Protection:** Intelligent deactivation safeguards ensure an instructor cannot be removed until all active students and past reports have been safely reassigned to a designated colleague.
- **Comprehensive Activity Audit Trail:** Detailed audit logging records significant administrative actions (staff invitations, student transfers, role modifications, and account terminations) for institutional transparency and compliance.

---

### 8. White-Labeling & Multi-Language Support
- **Custom Brand Identity:** Customize the platform’s public-facing and internal identity:
  - Institution and school name across all navigation bars, browser titles, and parent portals.
  - Custom institution logo and favicon across all viewports.
  - Custom administrative contact details and support WhatsApp channels.
- **Personalized Instructor Profiles:** Teachers can set their preferred professional greeting (e.g., "Sensei", "Teacher", "Coach", "Kak") and a customized closing signature that automatically signs off every report.
- **Native Bilingual Dashboard:** Seamlessly toggle between **English** and **Bahasa Indonesia** across both the entire dashboard interface and the generated student reports.
- **Sleek Light & Dark Modes:** Fully designed dark and light themes optimized for comfortable use during daytime classes or late-night administrative reviews.
- **Automated Image Optimization:** Built-in image compression ensures lesson photos upload instantly even on slower mobile connections, keeping the platform fast and responsive anywhere.
